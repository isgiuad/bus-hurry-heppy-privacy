Bus Hurry Heppy!

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026 · Android package: com.busfever.game

This policy describes the Android version of Bus Hurry Heppy! The game content is bundled inside the app and runs locally in an embedded WebView; it does not require a separately hosted game website.

Information stored on your device

The game stores progress, level state, coins, unlocks, preferences, local leaderboard scores, and incomplete rewarded-ad coin-offer progress in the app's local storage. This information stays on your device and is not uploaded by the game's local save system. It remains until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. The app does not provide cloud synchronization or cross-device recovery.

Advertising and consent

The app uses Google AdMob to provide interstitial and rewarded advertisements. Google's User Messaging Platform may present consent choices where required before advertisements are requested. Google and its advertising partners may process data such as IP-derived approximate location, ad views and interactions, diagnostics, performance information, consent signals, and device or advertising identifiers where permitted. These data may be used for advertising, measurement, security, and fraud prevention.

Learn more in the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Data we do not request

The app does not require an account and does not intentionally request your name, email address, contacts, camera, microphone, precise GPS location, payment details, chat messages, or user-generated content. Platform and advertising SDKs may still process technical data as described above.

Security and retention

Advertising traffic is transmitted using encrypted connections provided by Google. Local game data is protected by the Android app sandbox. Advertising providers retain data under their own policies. Local progress remains until app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled.

Your choices

  • You may decline or adjust consent choices when Google's consent form is shown, where available.
  • You may reset or limit advertising identifiers through Android settings.
  • You may delete local progress through Android Settings → Apps → Bus Hurry Heppy! → Storage → Clear storage, or by uninstalling the app.

Children

The current version is intended for a general audience and is not configured as a child-directed app. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of the app and device-level advertising controls.

Third-party services

The app uses Google Mobile Ads and Google User Messaging Platform. This privacy-policy page is hosted by GitHub Pages, so GitHub may receive standard web request information when you visit this page. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Policy changes

We may update this policy when features, advertising practices, or legal requirements change. The current effective date is shown at the top of this page.

Contact
